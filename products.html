Vanilla and chocolate layer cakes with buttercream start at $30. Standard cakes range from $30 to $65 depending on size and decoration. Tell us your guest count and occasion for a custom estimate.

Plan ahead for pickup

Please request breads and pastry boxes at least two days ahead, and cakes at least five days ahead. Requests need confirmation before we can reserve a pickup time or confirm a price.

Tell us about allergies when you inquire. Our kitchen handles wheat, milk, eggs, nuts, and sesame; ask about ingredients before ordering.
