Our story

North Star Bakery began with a family bread recipe and a weekend table at the neighborhood market. Regular customers asked for a place to stop during the week, and our little shop grew from there.

Today we bake for morning commuters, family meals, and celebrations. We want every visit to feel like a familiar stop on your way home.

Ingredients with a purpose

We start with flour, water, salt, and time. We look for regional flour suppliers and choose seasonal fruit from nearby growers when it is available. Small batches help us bake for the day and keep waste down.

Have an ingredient question? Ask our team before placing a request so we can discuss the recipe and your needs.
